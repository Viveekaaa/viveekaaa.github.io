Tiny Whys

The science hiding in everyday things.

Tiny Whys is a YouTube channel of short, fact-checked videos that explain the science behind everyday things: your kitchen, your body, your home, the weather and the phone in your pocket.

About the "faceless-shorts" app

faceless-shorts is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Tiny Whys channel. It uploads the channel's own videos to YouTube using the YouTube Data API, with permission to upload videos only. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access other people's accounts or data.
